Put Zee Leads on your home screen

It opens the team CRM in one tap, like an app.

iPhone (use Safari)

  1. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow) at the bottom.
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add. The blue Zee icon appears on your home screen.

Android (use Chrome)

  1. Tap the ⋮ menu at the top right.
  2. Tap Add to Home screen (or Install app).
  3. Tap Add.

Get your reminders

Every morning at 8am you get an email with your calls for the day, and at 1pm a reminder for any still open. Turn on notifications for the Gmail app so these pop up on your phone.
